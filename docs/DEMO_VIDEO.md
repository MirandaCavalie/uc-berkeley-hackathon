# Fieldwork demo video

## Purpose

Show the product as a working research instrument in under 75 seconds. The video should make the sequence obvious: choose a defensible sample, interview an employee, preserve evidence, and turn it into a roadmap decision.

## Shot list

| Time | Screen | Action | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–06s | Landing | Hold on the Fieldwork mark and promise | Make work visible. Make AI useful. |
| 06–15s | Discovery | Scroll to the control graph | Eight roles. One evidence trail. |
| 15–25s | Discovery | Show sample, team coverage, and quality signals | Start with the right people. |
| 25–42s | Interview | Start an invited employee, accept scope, answer the first questions | Ask about the work, not opinions about AI. |
| 42–52s | Interview | Answer a vague friction question and show the concrete-example probe | Weak evidence gets a follow-up. |
| 52–63s | Discovery | Submit and open the captured record | Every finding keeps its source. |
| 63–72s | Opportunities | Expand the first ranked opportunity | Choose automation, skill, agent, or human. |
| 72–78s | Roadmap | End on the phased roadmap and human checkpoint | A roadmap people can act on. |

## Recording direction

- Record at 1440×900 or 1920×1080 with the browser chrome hidden.
- Use the seeded Northwind workspace for a reliable walkthrough, then show the “seeded run” label once so the demo does not imply real employee data.
- Move slowly enough for the node labels and source quote to be readable.
- Use one light click sound per transition, no background music over the interview answer.
- Keep cursor movement intentional. Avoid hovering over every control.
- Export as H.264 MP4, 30 fps, with a 78 second maximum.

## Voiceover

“Fieldwork starts with the work, not a list of AI tools. It samples each team, interviews employees in short focused sessions, and keeps the source answer beside every finding. If an answer is vague, the interviewer asks for a recent example. Quality review sends weak evidence back for a follow-up. Only then does Fieldwork rank the opportunity and show what should stay human.”

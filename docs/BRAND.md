# Fieldwork brand

## Name

**Fieldwork.** Researchers do fieldwork: they go where the work happens and ask the people doing it. That is the product. It is also, literally, about work.

## Promise

**Make work visible. Make AI useful.**

One-liner: Fieldwork listens to the people doing the work, finds the friction between teams, and turns it into a defensible AI roadmap.

## Beliefs

1. **Ask the people who do the work.** Leaders see the org chart; employees see where the hours go.
2. **Every number traces to a quote.** No invented savings. Hours on the roadmap link to the person who reported them.
3. **Not everything should be an agent.** The right tool for each process: automation, skill, agent, or nothing.
4. **Human by default where judgment matters.** If people say it should stay human, the roadmap listens.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. We sound like a good researcher, not a hype deck.

- Say "hours back per month", not "unlock productivity".
- Say "the AP specialist retypes every invoice line", not "manual processes create friction".
- Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No italics for emphasis.

**Logo:** four field lines and an apricot signal point. The lines represent work mapped across teams; the point is the finding worth acting on.

- `Mark`: iris field lines, apricot dot. Use on light backgrounds; `inverted` for dark.
- `Logo`: white field lines on an iris rounded tile. App icon, favicon, interviewer avatar.
- `Wordmark`: mark plus "Fieldwork" in Geist semibold, tight tracking (-0.04em). Text height is 0.72 of the mark size.
- Clear space: half the mark size on every side. Never recolor the dot, outline the mark, or set the name in another typeface.

Files: `src/components/brand.tsx`, `src/app/icon.svg`, `src/app/apple-icon.tsx`, `src/app/opengraph-image.tsx` (fonts in `src/assets/fonts`).

**Type:** Geist, regular for body copy and medium for display. Avoid italic display text, all-caps paragraphs, and decorative gradients.

## Positioning

| | Consulting discovery | AI tools first | Fieldwork |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who is asked | 20 people, sampled | Nobody | Everyone |
| Time to insight | 3–6 months | Immediate, but blind | One week |
| Output | A slide deck | A license | A ranked roadmap with blueprints |
| Updates | Never | n/a | Re-interview every quarter |

Reference: [Horizon](https://usehorizon.ai/) proves enterprises buy AI-led continuous discovery (Mercado Libre, Despegar, PwC). Fieldwork's angle is the decision step: it tells you which kind of AI fits each process, with calibrated confidence, and which work to leave alone.
